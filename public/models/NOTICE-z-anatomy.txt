Anatomical reference mesh provenance

The z_anatomy_reference.glb file is derived from Z-Anatomy, the libre 3D atlas
of anatomy, licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0. Its models include derivatives of
BodyParts3D, The Database Center for Life Science, licensed CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan.

Attribution requested by the source project:
"BodyParts3D - The Database Center for Life Science - CC-BY-SA 2.1 Japan"
"Z-Anatomy - The libre 3D atlas of anatomy - CC-BY-SA 4.0"

Source: https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models-of-human-anatomy
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

This exported reference mesh remains under CC BY-SA 4.0. It is static and
does not supply a rig, muscle paths, physiological parameters, or force data.

The unified_muscle_surfaces.glb and unified_muscle_skin.json/
unified_muscle_weights.bin files are further registered and skinned derivatives
of this reference geometry. They remain under CC BY-SA 4.0. Their animation
uses the separately attributed OpenSim model; this derivative is not a source
of validated tissue deformation or muscle force.

The unified_anatomy_bones.glb and unified_anatomy_bones.json files are also
registered derivatives of the same Z-Anatomy source, under CC BY-SA 4.0.
They are display geometry driven by the OpenSim body transforms, not a second
kinematic or force model.
