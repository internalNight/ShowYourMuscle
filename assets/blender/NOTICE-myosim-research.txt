The local myosim_fullbody_structural_review.blend and PNG are generated from
the compiled MyoSim full-body model. They are ignored by Git and are not part
of the mobile app. MyoSim's arm documentation says MyoArm derives from the
MoBL upper-extremity model. A published MoBL model copy states non-commercial
terms and requires a separate license for commercial use:
https://github.com/CEINMS-RT/UpperLimbModel#license

The relationship between the MyoSim repository license and upstream model
rights has not been resolved. Do not ship or publish the generated files or
use them in a commercial product until the model rights are clarified.

The 416 muscle paths in the Blender file are static runtime-position
polylines. They do not represent validated muscle force, activation, or
personalized anatomy.
