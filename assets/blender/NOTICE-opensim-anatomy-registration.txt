The opensim_anatomy_registration.blend, opensim_anatomy_bone_motion.blend,
opensim_rectus_mesh_motion.blend, opensim_thigh_mesh_motion.blend,
opensim_thigh_mesh_62cm.blend,
and opensim_attachment_review.blend files
contain selected right-leg Z-Anatomy / BodyParts3D anatomical meshes.
The anatomical mesh derivative
remains under CC BY-SA 4.0. Attribution and source details are in
../../public/models/NOTICE-z-anatomy.txt.

They also overlay joint and muscle-path geometry derived from the OpenSim
exampleSquatToStand model. The OpenSim model credits and license are in
../opensim/NOTICE.txt.

The standing overlay uses a provisional translation of mesh bounding-box
centers. The second file moves six bones with OpenSim body transforms
through 101 frames. The third also adds a heuristic deformation of the right
rectus femoris mesh. The fourth adds the right biceps femoris short head and
semimembranosus using the same provisional deformation method. The 62-cm
variant scales their cross sections around the reference femur axis as a
visual girth scenario, without identifying individual muscle volume.
The last contains eight candidate muscle meshes and
source-model point markers for review. Patella and calcaneus have no independent
landmark registration. They are research alignment views,
not a validated anatomical rig or force model.
