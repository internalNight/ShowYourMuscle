The squatToStand_3dof9musc.osim model was taken from the OpenSim project's
exampleSquatToStand directory. Its embedded credits state that it is a
Rajagopal 2016 model reduced to 3 degrees of freedom and 9 muscles, using
maximum isometric forces from Carmichael Ong's gait8dof18musc model.

Source: https://github.com/opensim-org/opensim-core/tree/main/Bindings/Java/Matlab/examples/Moco/exampleSquatToStand
Repository license: Apache License 2.0
https://github.com/opensim-org/opensim-core/blob/main/LICENSE.txt

The derived squat_opensim_reference.json contains kinematic joint positions,
muscle path point positions, path lengths and moment arms at our prototype
poses. It does not contain solved muscle forces or activations and has not
been validated for individual users. Displayed polylines approximate the
model's computed path lengths to within 0.9 mm across the 101 sample poses.

The arm26.osim research candidate was taken from the OpenSim project's
opensim-models repository, Models/Arm26/arm26.osim. Its embedded credits
identify the OpenSim Development Team (Reinbolt, Seth, Habib, Hamner), adapted
from Kate Holzbaur's model, and state Creative Commons Attribution 3.0.
Source: https://github.com/opensim-org/opensim-models/blob/master/Models/Arm26/arm26.osim
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
The derived public/data/press_arm26_reference.json contains a synthetic angle
sweep with kinematic path points, path lengths and moment arms only. The phone
app displays this as a limited right-arm reference; it contains no solved
muscle forces or activation, and no personal scaling or barbell dynamics.
